Moyra UK — From Engagement to Conversion
A digital marketing audit and growth strategy for the UK's exclusive distributor of Moyra Hungary nail art products.

The Brief

As Project Manager for a 5-person MSc consultancy team, I led research into Moyra UK's social media performance and website/customer journey, diagnosing why a smaller, more engaged brand was under-leveraging its position against larger competitors.

What I Did

  • Ran technical and content audits using Semrush, PageSpeed Insights, SEO Site Checkup, and GTmetrix

Moyra UK's Instagram engagement rate (2.58%) was 23× higher than Crystal Nails UK's (0.11%) — despite Crystal Nails having a larger following. The real opportunity wasn't reach, it was conversion.

Qualitative Research · Researcher & Interviewer
PizzaExpress — Diagnosing the Service Quality Gap
An interpretivist study into how wait time, food consistency, and staff behaviour shape perceived value in UK casual dining.
View case study ↓

The Brief

Working in a 4-person team, I helped investigate why PizzaExpress was facing recurring complaints about service delays, food inconsistency, and staff behaviour, and what that meant for value perception and food waste.

What I Did

  • Triangulated secondary evidence from Trustpilot and TripAdvisor against primary interview data
  • Conducted 2 of 8 semi-structured interviews with students who had dined at PizzaExpress in the past 12 months
  • Applied SERVQUAL and Expectation-Disconfirmation Theory to structure the interview guide and interpret findings
  • Contributed to reflexive thematic analysis identifying three core themes across the dataset

Key Finding

Dissatisfaction wasn't driven by wait time alone — it was driven by uncommunicated wait time. Food quality inconsistency also linked directly to avoidable food waste, tying the commercial problem to UN SDG 12.
