Moyra UK — From Engagement to Conversion
A digital marketing audit and growth strategy for the UK's exclusive distributor of Moyra Hungary nail art products.
View case study ↓

The Brief

As Project Manager for a 5-person MSc consultancy team, I led the research into Moyra UK's social media performance and website/customer journey, diagnosing why a smaller, more engaged brand was under-leveraging its position against larger competitors.

What I Did

  • Ran technical and content audits using Semrush, Google PageSpeed Insights, SEO Site Checkup, and GTmetrix
  • Analysed Instagram and TikTok performance data via Socialinsider against a named competitor, Crystal Nails UK
  • Directed the social media and website research strands across the team, and managed timeline, allocation, and deliverable quality
  • Translated audit findings into a client-facing PDF report and a 6-tab Excel analysis workbook
  • Built and delivered a 52-slide strategy presentation in Canva, including full speaker notes

Key Finding

Moyra UK's Instagram engagement rate (2.58%) was 23× higher than Crystal Nails UK's (0.11%) — despite Crystal Nails having a larger following. The real opportunity wasn't reach, it was conversion: turning an already-engaged audience into buyers, which reframed the whole strategy.

Qualitative Research · Researcher & Interviewer
PizzaExpress — Diagnosing the Service Quality Gap
An interpretivist study into how wait time, food consistency, and staff behaviour shape perceived value in UK casual dining.
View case study ↓

The Brief

Working in a 4-person team for the MSc Marketing Research module, I helped investigate why PizzaExpress — despite strong brand recognition — was facing recurring customer complaints about service delays, food inconsistency, and staff behaviour, and what that meant for value perception and food waste.

What I Did

  • Sourced and triangulated secondary evidence from Trustpilot and TripAdvisor reviews against primary interview data
  • Conducted 2 of 8 semi-structured interviews with NTU students who had dined at PizzaExpress in the past 12 months
  • Applied SERVQUAL and Expectation-Disconfirmation Theory to structure the interview guide and interpret findings
  • Contributed to reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke, 2006) identifying three core themes across the dataset

Key Finding

Dissatisfaction wasn't driven by wait time alone — it was driven by uncommunicated wait time. Customers tolerated real delays when staff explained them, but silence turned manageable inconvenience into perceived negligence. Food quality inconsistency also linked directly to avoidable food waste, tying the commercial problem to UN SDG 12.

Recommendation

A standardised Service Quality and Communication Protocol across UK branches — mandatory delay communication, dispatch-point quality checkpoints, and frontline training grounded in Hochschild's (1983) emotional labour framework — to rebuild trust without requiring a menu or pricing change.
